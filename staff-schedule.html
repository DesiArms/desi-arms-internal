Staff Schedule

What to work on, day by day

Every day

Start and end every shift like this

When you get infirst 30 min

  • Clock in on the Time Tracker
  • Open the Task Manager and see if anything new came in or is late
  • Walk the store: straighten the shelves, note anything that's sold out, wipe down the cases

All day

  • Customers come first. If someone walks in, stop what you're doing and help them.
  • If you're not sure about something, ask. Don't guess on anything with a serial number on it.

Before you leavelast 15 min

  • Clock out
  • Leave three quick lines in the Task Manager: what you finished, what you got stuck on, what you need from Glenn
Clock in Open tasks

Monday

Order stock, check in deliveries

Build the order listabout 2 hr

  • Look at what sold over the weekend
  • Make a list of what we need to restock, and which supplier to get it from — Lipsey's, Sports South, RSR, Zanders or Davidson's
  • Send the list to Glenn
  • Once he says yes, place the orders and write down what you ordered and when it should arrive
Don't place any order until Glenn approves it. Build the list, send it, wait.

Check in deliveriesabout 1 hr

  • Open the boxes and make sure everything on the packing slip is actually in there
  • Set any firearms aside — those get entered in the book tomorrow

Store upgrade projectabout 1 hr

  • Pick up the next step from the project list at the bottom of this page

Social media

  • Write all three of this week's posts now and schedule them, so you're not scrambling later in the week
Log time Open tasks

Tuesday

Tag it, log it, set up the laser

Tag and logabout 2 hr

  • Print price tags for everything that came in Monday on the Zebra label printer
  • Enter every firearm into the bound book in Corestore
  • Check each one against the packing slip as you go — serial number, make, model
  • Put the regular stock out on the shelves and the firearms in the case
The bound book lives in Corestore. You may hear FastBound mentioned — we're not on it yet, so don't enter anything there.

Get the laser readyabout 2 hr

  • Clean up this week's artwork in Illustrator, then set the layers in LightBurn
  • Set up the holder that keeps the part in the same spot every time, and check your focus on the surface you're marking
  • Line parts up off the flat, solid parts — the spine of a blade or the flat end of a magazine. Never off a curved edge or a tip.
  • Look up your settings in the Laser Library and make sure you have the right lens on
Only run settings marked "Proven." A new material, a different color plastic, or anything that's a display piece goes to Glenn. Don't touch Damascus at all.
Log time Open tasks Laser Library

Wednesday

Email day

Check the automatic emails30–45 min

  • We have two email series that send on their own — one for Intro to Firearms, one for CCW. Each is three emails: one right away, one 3 days later, one 7 days after signup.
  • Make sure people are still getting added. They come in four ways: range signups, store signups, the website form, and past class students.
  • Look at how many people opened each email, clicked, or unsubscribed. If one email is doing way worse than the others, flag it.
  • Check the timing is still set to "wait 3 days after they sign up," not a specific calendar date.
  • Emails should be going out Tuesday through Thursday, between 9 and 10 in the morning.

This week's email1–1.5 hr

  • Write one email to the whole list and send it the same day
  • Pick a different topic than the class emails cover, so people signed up for those don't get two similar emails in a row
You don't need this approved. Write it and send it.

Thursdaybusiest day

Customer jobs and engraving

Engraving and work ordersabout 3 hr
