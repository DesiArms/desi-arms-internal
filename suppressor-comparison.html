How to read this

IndependentSound figure from a third-party lab (PEW Science / published shoot-off). Most trustworthy and comparable.
Mfr. claimedNumber published by the maker on their own rig. Useful, but not directly comparable across brands.
No data yetNo independent or published dB figure. Judge on design intent, not numbers.
HUBUniversal 1.375×24 rear thread. Swap any HUB mount or direct-thread adapter across rifles.
ProprietaryBrand-specific mount. Needs that brand's muzzle device on each host.
FlowFlow-through / low back pressure — less gas-to-face and easier cycling on semi-autos.
Booster9mm only: a tilting-barrel pistol (most handguns) needs a booster / piston to cycle. Fixed-barrel pistols and PCCs don't. Some cans include the piston; some sell it separately.

About the sound numbers: decibel and suppression figures come from different test setups, hosts, and ammunition, so treat them as directional rather than head-to-head. For rifles, the most comparable data is from PEW Science, measured 1 meter left of the muzzle; on supersonic .30 cal every can lands within a few decibels, so the real separators are back pressure, mounting, weight, and durability, not raw dB. Subsonic 9mm is quieter overall but the same caveat applies — judge on design and fit, not a single number. No suppressor makes a supersonic centerfire host "hearing safe"; eye and ear protection is still recommended.
